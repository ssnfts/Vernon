## References

- A.fea.beam_deflection: Euler-Bernoulli cantilever tip deflection P L^3/(3 E I) (Roark, Formulas for Stress and Strain, Table 8.1 case 1a); Timoshenko shear adds ~0.8% at this L/h
- A.fea.beam_stress: Flexure formula sigma = M y / I with M = P (L/2) at mid-span (any strength-of-materials text)
- A.fea.patch_uniaxial: Zienkiewicz & Taylor, patch test: a constant stress state must be reproduced by consistent elements
- A.geo.sphere_area_volume: Closed-form sphere; inscribed UV polyhedron error shrinks with refinement
- A.geo.plane_cylinder: Plane A = a b; regular n-gon: area 1/2 n r^2 sin(2 pi/n), edge 2 r sin(pi/n); exact for the tessellation
- A.clean.icp_known_transform: Besl & McKay 1992 / Chen & Medioni 1992; ground truth is the applied transform
- A.clean.qem_bounds: Garland & Heckbert 1997; independent check against the analytic sphere and the input mesh
- A.flatten.developable_cylinder: A cylinder is developable (Gaussian curvature 0): the flat pattern is arc length x width with zero strain (do Carmo, Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces)
- A.flatten.sphere_cap_distorts: Gauss's Theorema Egregium: K != 0 means no isometric flat map exists
- A.print.snap_fit_beam: Rectangular constant-section cantilever: curvature at root gives eps = c kappa with y = kappa L^2 / 3, so eps = 1.5 t y / L^2 (standard snap-fit design relation)
