# k2 review: solar, flatten, performance (vernon-nodes-solar incl. flatten, vernon-nodes-perf)

Vetted 2026-10-22 against the real Vernon API (vernon-core geometry.rs, report.rs, attrib.rs; vernon-graph node.rs, param.rs, data.rs, graph.rs, registry.rs; CLEAN_ROOM.md; docs/specs/solar-thermal.md, performance-validation.md, rhino8-mesh-tools.md). Crate facts come from `cargo info` plus the unpacked crate sources in the local registry. No web searches were used: none of the 79 claims cites a case, court or year, so the only legal content is licence strings, and those were read from `cargo info`. Everything marked "verified here" was re-computed in throw-away scripts (pure Python for the maths, scratch Rust crates for rayon and mimalloc, none kept in the repo). Anything not re-checked is marked UNVERIFIED. k2 saw no Vernon code, so its structure (ModifierNode, MetaInfo, fingerprint(), FaceField, Python API) does not exist here; its maths is partly right and its code is mostly broken.

## 1. Summary

1. Vernon already has what k2 re-invents: attributes live in `Geometry` (so a flatten or thermal node cannot "drop" a field), nodes are stateless `NodeOp`, and the graph already caches per node on a hash of type, params and upstream signatures. Reject k2's node scaffolding, caching and Python API; keep its maths ideas.
2. The solar chain is already specified better in docs/specs/solar-thermal.md (Tregenza 145-patch sky, cumulative sky matrix, BVH occlusion, bisection-safe surface balance, material heat table). k2's solar numbers disagree with that spec in ways that flip verdicts (its sky offset and convection law give 93.8 degC for the black still-air panel where the spec gives 104.7, which moves ASA from fail to ok). Use the spec; take only k2's correct EPW column indices and its bug list.
3. Flatten: k2's "LSCM" is a scalar Laplace solve (verified here: v is identically 0). A correct LSCM (complex per-triangle residual, 2n real unknowns, two pins) reproduces an isometry on a developable strip to 1e-13. Pinning at the 3-D chord distance does not give mm scale (verified: 0.761 ratio); rescale to the 3-D area instead. Closed meshes need a real vertex-duplicating seam cut first.
4. Performance: k2's lap-sim critique is largely right (no grip limit, no braking, ClA unused, zero-speed stall, rpm without gear ratio, calibration overwrite, exponential synthetic test). The spec's forward-backward solver and test numbers (skidpad 6.3975 s, stadium 35.896 s) already cover all of it. One k2 "improvement" is a hazard: a through-origin coast-down fit with Crr fixed 25 percent wrong biases CdA by -19.9 percent (verified).
5. Dependencies: reject nalgebra, sprs (+sprs-ldl, LGPL-2.1), sysinfo, num_cpus, once_cell, chrono, image, rand, argmin for now; k2's `mimalloc 0.2` does not exist (0.1.52, C code, optional, P3); faer 0.24.4 (MIT, pure Rust) is optional behind a feature and needs MSRV 1.84 (workspace says 1.80, toolchain is 1.99.1). rayon 1.12.0 is the only must-have, and `par_iter().sum()` is not reproducible (verified: 18 to 19 distinct f64 results in 20 runs at 4 to 16 threads), so reductions use fixed chunks.

Verdict counts over 79 claims: ACCEPT 31, ADAPT 38, REJECT 10.

## 3. Bugs and hazards in k2's code and claims

Maths and physics
- Sun vector: `z = sin(el)` with a dot against `-S` zeroes every upward face; `(cos az, sin az)` puts az 180 at west (verified). Elsewhere z = `-sin(zenith)` and radians/degrees mixed. Only the section 4.1 form (toward the sun, z = cos zenith) is usable.
- Sun position: JD missing the /86400, `cos` for `sin` in declination, equation of time subtracts declination, longitude unused, azimuth divides by sin(dec) (zero at equinox), `Utc::now()` and year 2022 hard-coded (Feb 29 panics), chrono deprecated constructors.
- "LSCM": scalar Laplace, v = 0 identically (verified); unit-square normalisation divides by epsilon; pins by index; cot weights on the wrong edge, wrong sign at b, diagonal and pin loop absent.
- Pin-at-3-D-distance does not fix scale (0.761 verified); rescale by sqrt(A3/A2) instead.
- Sky dome: cosine-of-elevation solid angle (ring weights inverted, 7.6x too large for the 75 to 90 deg band), bands 7.5 to 97.5 deg (7.05 sr), horizon band missing, diffuse sum without Omega, night guard inside a comment.
- Ground term: (GHI + DHI) x albedo x abs(n_z) gives horizontal faces the most ground light and double counts DHI.
- Constants: Tsky = Ta - 6 and h = 10.45 - v + 10 sqrt(v) shift the black still-air panel by 11 K (verified), enough to flip the ASA verdict.
- HDT misread as an hourly series; EPW code reads wrong columns, tz as i32, guard of 50 fields rejects every row, subtracts 273.15 from a degC value.
- Memory: 8760 x faces matrices (700.8 GB for 10M faces, verified) are unnecessary with a cumulative sky matrix and streaming accumulators.
- Benchmarks: 1 ns per face-hour and "3 ms per hour per face" cannot both be true; neither is plausible.
- Lap sim: no grip limit or braking, ClA unused, zero-speed stall, wheel rpm instead of engine rpm, calibration overwrites the aero map, wrong monotonicity test, exponential synthetic coast-down, `dyn` as an identifier.
- Coast-down "through-origin" improvement is only valid with an exactly known Crr (-19.9 percent CdA at +25 percent Crr error, verified).
- ARAP: per-vertex covariance needs 2-D local frames; the 2x2 rotation needs no SVD.

Engineering and architecture
- `spawn_fifo` returns `()`, joins on it do not compile, and blocking after each spawn serialises the graph; `?` on a join error aborts the run (contradicts the panic test). Graph signatures must be computed before cooking, so parallelism belongs in a wave scheduler.
- `rayon::par_iter().sum()` on f64 is not reproducible (verified, 18 to 19 distinct results per 20 runs at 4 to 16 threads). Golden tests and cache equality need fixed-chunk reductions.
- Rayon and mimalloc both work on this machine (rayon 1.12.0 ran on x86_64-pc-windows-gnu; mimalloc 0.1.52 compiled with the WinLibs MinGW), so "disable the pool on Windows" is unnecessary.
- `Arc::make_mut` on a multi-million-triangle `Geometry` clones points, prims and every attribute even for an attribute-only change (solar, flatten). Peak memory is several copies of the mesh; not fixed by k2's chunking. Core redesign (Arc'd columns) is out of scope here; note for later.
- No memory cap on `Graph::cache`.
- Clean-room: k2 names a flag `use_ladybug_sky`, and talks about "Squish" and OptimumLap-style behaviour. Vendor names may appear in docs, specs and `emulates`, never in labels, help or enum options.
- UNVERIFIED: attribution of the Tsky and convection formulas, 3MF property sections, patent status of LSCM and ARAP (not checked; Vernon implements published papers cited in the specs), k2's speed-up and memory-saving figures, the Perez/H-DKR labelling. No case law is involved.
