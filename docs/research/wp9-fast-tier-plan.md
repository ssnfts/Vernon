# WP9 fast tier: validation tests that finish in one hour on this PC

Status: **plan in execution, 2026-11-11.** User direction: the multi-week
schedule in `wp9-ddes-route-plan.md` section 7 is rejected; every validation

## 1. What one hour can and cannot certify (stated once, up front)

- **Cannot:** the v1 contract (`wp9-replication-baseline-v1.json`), which is
  the road-car benchmark on a 12M-cell half model. One steady iteration costs
  18.7 s there and one DDES step 32 s (measured). No gate, time step or
  numerics make that finish in an hour on 16 ranks. v1 stays on record as
  unmet; nothing below passes it or relabels it.
- **Can:** a **fast tier**: bluff bodies of about 0.5M cells, time-resolved
  DDES under the v2 transient acceptance, scored against references whose
  method and setup are published in full. At that size one DDES step is about
  1 s, one convective unit (L/U) is about 0.03 s, and the whole v2 run
  (wash-out, averaging to the stop rule, Δt/2 check) is minutes.

The fast tier is a **new contract**, `wp9-fast-tier-v1.json`, chosen by the
user. Its rows are reported with their own method label and interval, never
as v1 results.

## 3. Budget of one test (hard limit 60 min, enforced by the harness)

| Step | Budget | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Mesh (~0.5M cells, 16 ranks) | ≤ 5 min | 2026-10-31 runs |
| Steady start (≤ 600 iterations) | ≤ 8 min | ~0.6 s/iteration scaled from 3.76M at 4.44 s |
| DDES: 2 CTU wash-out + 6–15 CTU averaging | ≤ 35 min | ~1–1.5 s/step, Δt from wake Courant ≈ 1 |
| Δt/2 segment (2.5 CTU) | ≤ 10 min | twice the step count over a quarter of the time |
| Gate, report | seconds | |

If the budget runs out before the gate passes, the row is **fail (budget)**,
never a pass, and the averaging so far is reported with its interval.

## 4. Acceptance (fast tier v1; the v2 transient rules at small scale)

Unchanged from the v2 draft in `wp9-ddes-route-plan.md` section 3.1:
wash-out ≥ 2 CTU; averaging ≥ 6 CTU, checked every 1 CTU, cap 15 CTU;
time-weighted mean; trend and halves tests fail only when significant (99%)
and large (0.5%/CTU, 0.2%); ESS ≥ 50; standard error ≤ 0.5% of the mean as
the larger of the autocorrelation and batch-means estimates; per-step rule
(p final ≤ 1e-4; U, k, omega drop ≥ 1 order across the outer loops in ≥ 99%
of steps); a Δt/2 segment whose mean agrees within the combined interval.

Band: 5% on Cd for both reference kinds, stated as such. A reference from a
20M–300M-cell LES compared with a 0.5M-cell DDES will often miss the band;
that is reported as the error it is. The classic body's slant share is a
soft check (G25), unchanged.

## 5. Steps in execution order

1. **Downloads** (running in the background; `scratch/wp9-data/*`): licence,
   tables, five geometries each for the slant and Windsor variants, the
   slant dataset's case-setup archive.
2. **`transient.rs`** (new, pure): time-weighted statistics, the stop rule,
   the per-step rule from the solver log, the Δt/2 comparison. Test-first.
3. **Recipe** (`foam.rs`, `case.rs`): LUST convection for U; k and omega on
   every outer loop with outer residual control; the D5 gradient limiters; a
   fixed-Δt mode; averaged fields written from the wash-out end; a restart
   from the steady solution. Test-first.
4. **Harness** `real_openfoam_fast_tier_run` (environment-driven, like the
   slant-body and road-car harnesses): mesh → steady start → DDES segments
   under the stop rule → Δt/2 → gate → JSON row with the reference and its
   method label. Budget enforced inside.
5. **First test:** classic slant body 25° at 40 m/s, dx0 0.092 (~0.5M cells),
   16 ranks, one hour. Then 35°.
6. **Dataset rows:** slant variants 1–5 and Windsor 1 at their published
   conditions, one hour each, one at a time.
7. **Checks and record:** `cargo build/test/clippy`, demo, NODES.md, app
   launch; `fast-tier-v1.json`; CONTINUATION; ROADMAP's "transient DES not
   planned" line corrected.

Follow-ups, not in this pass: a `wp9-fast` runner profile (the first tests
run through a labelled wrapper script, as the 2026-10-31 slant-body runs
did); dataset rows as `benchmark_check` presets; the road-car rows.
